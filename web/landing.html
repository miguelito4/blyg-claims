Built on Blygger

A track record you own. Not one we rent you.

Blyggi runs on the Blygger protocol: self-hosted, versioned writing that anyone can read and follow. Blyggi is a front door to it. It keeps nothing.

You own it

Your claims live on your own blyg, not on Blyggi. Close Blyggi and every claim is still there.

Anyone can verify it

Every claim is pinned with a content hash. Anyone can recompute your record from public files, including a rival app.

Nobody can edit history

Change a claim later and readers see both versions. The pinned original is the one that's scored.

It goes where you go

Move your blyg and your record moves with it. No lock-in, no export request, no platform.

How it works

Four answers. Then it's on the record, for good.

  1. 1What kind of claim?Yes or no, from a list, how much, or just record it.
  2. 2What will happen?One sentence a stranger could check.
  3. 3By when?A date. Past it, the claim is due.
  4. 4How sure?A probability, a ranking, or a range.
Want rigor? Make it checkable by strangers.

Point the claim at a published statistic, an official record or a blyg: 18 publishers and 22 registers, from the World Bank to the EU's law database. The answer comes from the source, not your say-so.

The form argues back.

It warns you when a claim is already true, when the data won't be out by your date, when a word like "significant" means nothing, and when your percentages don't add up.

Why Blyggi

Your questions. Your record. Checkable by anyone.

Forecasting sites score you on their questions and keep your record on their servers. Blyggi scores the calls you actually make, and the record is yours.
